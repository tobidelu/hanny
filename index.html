About Us

Nestled in Adigbe, Abeokuta, Ogun State, Hanni Beauty Palace was created to bring personalized, luxurious beauty care to the community. We specialize in a full range of services, including organic skincare, makeup, lash and microblading, pedicure and manicure, facials, body treatments, and a curated store of cosmetics, perfumes and wellness products — all designed to enhance your natural radiance. Our team uses quality products and attentive, appointment-based care tailored to each client's unique style and needs. What sets us apart is our devotion to detail and the warm atmosphere we create, where beauty and confidence naturally come together. At Hanni Beauty Palace, every visit is an opportunity to take time for yourself, relax, and leave feeling renewed and graceful.
